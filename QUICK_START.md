# Quick Start: What This Prototype Does

This prototype is for Case Study 2: helping Malaysian students and fresh graduates avoid debt and build saving habits.

The idea is called **GX Resilience**. It is imagined as a new feature inside GXBank.

## Main Idea

Normal banking apps show what happened after you spend money.

GX Resilience helps before the problem gets worse. It checks spending patterns, warns the user, and gives one-click actions like saving money automatically or pausing risky spending.

## What Each Screen Means

| Screen | Meaning | What to Click |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Shows the student's money health today | `Check spending`, then `Save RM36` |
| AI Coach | Gives personal advice based on spending | Try Food, Shopping, or Payday |
| Auto Save | Shows automatic saving rules | `Simulate salary day` |
| Rewards | Turns good money habits into challenges | `Claim progress` |
| Friends | Lets friends save together privately | `Save RM10 with friends` |

## Easiest Presentation Script

Say this:

> Our solution is GX Resilience, a GXBank feature for students and fresh graduates. The problem is that young people often know they should save, but they do not act consistently. Our prototype uses AI nudges, automatic savings, and rewards to turn good financial behaviour into a daily habit.

Then demo:

1. On Home, point to the Resilience Score and Safe to Spend.
2. Click `Check spending`.
3. Click `Save RM36`.
4. Go to `Auto Save`.
5. Click `Simulate salary day`.
6. Go to `Rewards`.
7. Click `Claim progress`.

Closing line:

> The goal is to make financial resilience automatic, not something students only think about after they are already in debt.

## Important Features to Mention

- AI checks spending and gives personalized advice.
- Debt Shield warns users before spending becomes risky.
- Auto Save moves money into savings without relying on discipline.
- Rewards and streaks make good habits easier to continue.
- Friends can save together, but the app keeps exact balances private.
