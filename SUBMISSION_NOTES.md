# Submission Notes

## One-minute Pitch

GX Resilience makes financial resilience the default behaviour for students and fresh graduates. Instead of showing a passive transaction list, it converts each banking moment into a small action: save the difference, cool off risky spending, split salary into an Emergency Pocket, and keep habits alive through streaks and circles.

The prototype demonstrates how GXBank could layer AI and behavioural economics on top of existing banking interactions. The key idea is simple: users should not need perfect discipline. The bank should help them act at the right moment.

## Problem Framing

Malaysian youth often know they should save and avoid debt, but awareness does not reliably become action. Existing tools usually present balances and transactions after the fact. GX Resilience intervenes earlier with personalized nudges, automated money movement, and habit feedback loops.

## Demo Highlights

- Resilience Score combines cash buffer, debt exposure, savings progress, automation settings, and streaks.
- Debt Shield identifies risky discretionary or BNPL-style spending.
- AI Coach converts spending patterns into short, context-aware next steps.
- GX Pocket rules automate round-ups, payday splits, and cooling-off windows.
- Habit challenges and private circles support long-term consistency.

## Why This Is Original

Most finance apps stop at charts. GX Resilience is designed around behaviour change:

- Make the healthy action the easiest action.
- Reward consistency, not only large savings.
- Use automation before money feels available to spend.
- Keep social accountability private and low-pressure.
- Tie nudges directly to real banking moments.

## Suggested Closing

The goal is not to make students feel guilty about money. The goal is to make the resilient choice feel normal, timely, and almost automatic.
